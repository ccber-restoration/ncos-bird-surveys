# README

This subfolder is for a project compiling and visualizing _potential_ trophic networks based on bird species their prey recorded at North Campus Open Space.

Contacts: Francis Joyce and Gabriella Morales

# List of files:
- **aquatic_focal_species.csv** [not a good filename] preliminary list of aquatic bird species recorded in NCOS bird surveys (with total count detected). Only includes "waterfowl & friends" and "shorebirds" groups. 
- **invert_abundances.csv**: summary of invertebrate abundances based on sweepnet sampling data (see https://github.com/ccber-restoration/aquatic-invert-program). 
- **NCOS_aquatic_trophic_links_2026-02-10.xlsx**: contains trophic links between 9 focal taxa of aquatic invertebrates and bird species documented from North Campus Open Space monthly surveys that might be consuming those invertebrates. The bird taxa are primarily ducks (waterfowl & friends) and shorebirds. Trophic relationships were compiled from Birds of the World: https://birdsoftheworld.org/bow/home. This was downloaded as .xlsx from the working Google Sheet version.


- **NCOS_piscivorous_trophic_links_2026-02-17.xlsx**: contains trophic links between 4 focal taxa of larger prey items (fish, amphibians, an invasive crayfish) and bird species documented from North Campus Open Space monthly surveys that might be consuming those invertebrates. The bird taxa are primarily ducks (waterfowl & friends) and shorebirds. Trophic relationships were compiled from Birds of the World: https://birdsoftheworld.org/bow/home. This was downloaded as .xlsx from the working Google Sheet version.
